Day 5 – Time Series & Time series analysis

Today I learned about time series and time series analysis.

Unlike regular data, time series data is ordered by time e.g daily sales, monthly revenue, yearly population, stock prices, etc. The time element changes everything.

I learned the key components:
	- Trend: the long-term direction (upward or downward)
	- Seasonality: repeating patterns over fixed periods
	- Cyclical patterns: longer-term fluctuations
	- Noise: random variation
Time series analysis helps us understand these patterns and make forecasts.
